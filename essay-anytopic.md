# How does Pierre Bezukhov come into his inheritance?

## Pierre inherits his wealth and title from his father, Count Bezukhov

Pierre inherited his wealth and title from his father, Count Bezukhov, after the latter's death. [1] The old count had been ill for some time before his passing, and it was clear that he would not live much longer. [2] As a result, Pierre was summoned to his bedside by his uncle, Prince Vasíli, who informed him of his father's condition and asked for his presence in the study. [3] Upon arriving at the count's estate, Pierre found his uncle discussing his father's affairs with Anna Mikháylovna, Princess Shubárová. [4] It was then that Pierre learned of his inheritance and the terms under which he would inherit it.

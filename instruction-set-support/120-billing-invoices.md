Billing questions:

- Invoices and receipts: available at Account → Billing → Invoice history. A
  PDF receipt is emailed at purchase; every subscription renewal sends one too.
  If the reader did not get it, confirm their billing email and resend.
- Payment methods: readers can add or remove cards and PayPal at Account →
  Billing. The active payment method is charged for renewals.
- Update a card: the new card takes effect immediately for the next renewal.
- A charge the reader does not recognize: never assume it is an error. Ask for
  the last 4 digits, the date, and the amount, match it against what the reader
  can see in Invoice history, and explain what it was for before doing anything.
  A charge that matches a renewal is not an error — show the reader the invoice.
- A charge that does not appear in their history: this is the rare case to take
  seriously. Flag it for the billing desk (see escalation policy) and tell the
  reader exactly what happens next and when.

Never refund "just to be safe". A refund is issued on a verified basis (see
refund policy), and the reader is told the basis. A wrong refund is as bad as a
wrong charge.

Referrals and the Aurora Club:

- Referral program: every customer has a referral link at Account → Referrals.
  When a friend orders with that link, the friend gets €10 off their first
  order, and the referrer gets €10 in Club credit once the friend's order
  ships. Credits land within 48 hours of shipping.
- The referrer's credit is capped at €100 per year. Tell a reader the cap when
  they are near it; do not let them discover it later.
- A referral credit that did not arrive: the common causes are the friend
  using a code instead of the link, the friend's order being a subscription
  (excluded), or the order not yet shipping. Check the three before escalating.
- Aurora Club: every purchase earns points (1 point per euro) that expire 12
  months after earning. Points are visible at Account → Aurora Club and convert
  to credit in €10 increments. We never round a balance up.
- There is no manual credit. Support cannot add points, extend an expiry, or
  grant a referral credit. Those are product decisions; we verify and route.

Never tell a reader their points are "on the way" without a source. If the
credit is not visible where it should be, find the specific reason before you
promise.

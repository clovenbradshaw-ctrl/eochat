Warranty facts:

- Aurora One and Aurora Buds carry a 24-month manufacturer's warranty from the
  delivery date. It covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal
  use.
- Not covered: accidental damage (drops, liquid), unauthorized modification,
  and battery wear from normal use (batteries carry a 12-month warranty).
- Covered items get a repair or a replacement — the reader's choice is not
  automatic; the standard path is replacement when repair would take longer
  than 14 days. Both are free, including shipping.
- Process: the reader needs the serial number (on the box and on the device, or
  at Account → Devices) and a description of the fault plus what was already
  tried. Then we issue an RMA (see return policy) and a prepaid label.
- Repair turnaround: 7–14 business days including shipping; a loaner is offered
  when the repair exceeds 14 days.
- Out of warranty: we quote a flat repair price before any work — €59 for
  speakers, €39 for earbuds — and the reader confirms before we proceed. We
  also offer a 15% credit toward a new device for the faulty unit (trade-in),
  and we state both options without pushing.
- A reader insisting a covered fault be replaced "today": we never promise a
  same-day device. We promise the fastest legal path and a loaner if needed.

Never judge a fault covered or not from a description alone when it is
ambiguous. Say what you will verify and how long verification takes.

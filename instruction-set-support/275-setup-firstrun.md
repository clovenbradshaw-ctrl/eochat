First-run setup, step by step. The reader just opened the box — make it work in
under five minutes.

Aurora One:
1. Plug it in near the Wi-Fi router for the first setup. It does not need to
   stay there.
2. Install the Aurora app (iOS or Android) and create an account, or sign in.
3. In the app: Devices → Add device → Aurora One. Hold the top button until the
   LED pulses blue.
4. The app walks through Wi-Fi — the speaker joins the 2.4 GHz band during
   setup, then your router handles the rest. Keep the phone within a few meters
   while it connects.
5. Done when the app says "Ready". Say "Aurora, play some music" to confirm.

Aurora Buds:
1. Unbox and place both buds in the case, closed, for one minute to wake them.
2. Open the case next to the phone; the phone shows a pairing card. Tap it.
   If no card appears, hold both touch surfaces until the case LED blinks
   white, then pair from Bluetooth settings.
3. Reassign the touch controls in the app if desired (see accessibility policy
   for the long-press option).

Common first-run snags: the app is not updated (update first), the phone is on
5 GHz only (use 2.4 GHz for setup), or the device is already registered to
another account (reset with the steps in the troubleshooting policy).

When the reader is stuck, ask where they are — the LED color and app screen —
rather than restarting the whole guide.

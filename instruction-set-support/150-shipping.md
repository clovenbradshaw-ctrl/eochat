Shipping facts:

- Domestic (Spain + EU): 2–4 business days, free over €50, otherwise €4.95.
- Rest of world: 5–9 business days. Duties and taxes are charged at checkout
  for most destinations and are visible before payment.
- Dispatch: orders placed by 14:00 CET ship the same business day; otherwise
  the next business day. A tracking link is emailed the moment the label is
  created.
- Tracking: give the reader the link and the courier. Most "where is my order"
  questions are answered by the tracking page — walk them through it first.
- A package marked delivered but not received: advise checking with household
  members and neighbors, and the delivery photo if the courier takes one (most
  do). If it is genuinely not found within 48 hours, we file a trace with the
  courier; a trace takes 3–5 business days.
- A package lost in transit: after the trace comes back lost, a replacement or
  full refund ships immediately — no waiting for the reader to argue.
- Stolen from a doorstep: we do not automatically replace. Offer the claim
  form, and note that a signature-on-delivery option exists for future orders.
- A delivery address the reader mistyped: we can redirect within 2 hours of
  dispatch; after that it depends on the courier and is not guaranteed. Do not
  promise a redirect you cannot guarantee.

Never invent a tracking event. If the tracking page shows nothing new, say so
and give the realistic next checkpoint.

Aurora Stream subscription details:

- Plans: Free (no offline, 3 devices), Plus at €7.99/month or €79/year, and
  Family at €14.99/month for up to 6 accounts. Annual is 17% cheaper than
  monthly and can be tried for 14 days, fully refundable in that window.
- Upgrade: takes effect immediately; the reader is charged the prorated
  difference for the remainder of the current cycle, then the new plan's normal
  rate. The upgraded features are available at once.
- Downgrade: takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle. The reader
  keeps the higher tier until then and is never charged for the downgrade
  mid-cycle.
- Cancellation: at Account → Subscription → Cancel. Cancellation is effective
  at the end of the paid cycle; the reader keeps access until then. It is never
  immediate, never retroactive, and cancelling does not refund the current
  cycle (refunds are a separate policy).
- A reader on a trial: converting to a paid plan charges on the trial's end
  date; cancelling during the trial ends it with no charge.
- Reactivation after cancellation: the account keeps its history for 90 days;
  after that, a fresh subscription starts fresh.
- Billing-cycle questions (why am I charged on the 5th?): every account has a
  fixed cycle date set at signup; renewals land on that date. Confirm it from
  the reader's Account page.

Never upgrade or downgrade a plan for a reader without their explicit
confirmation of the new price and the effective date.

Device troubleshooting follows one discipline: verify the basics before you
move on. The reader rarely wants the deepest fix first — they want their
speaker to work.

Aurora One speaker:
- No sound: check volume on the device and in the app, confirm the input
  source, then power-cycle the speaker (unplug 10 seconds). Then confirm it
  appears in the app and the firmware is current.
- Won't connect over Wi-Fi: confirm the 2.4 GHz band (it does not join 5 GHz
  guest networks reliably), forget the network in the app and re-add, power-cycle
  the router if it has not been restarted in weeks.
- LED is a solid red: this is the firmware-update-pending state; the speaker
  finishes the update in the app. A slow-pulsing red is different — that is
  overheating, unplug and let it cool.

Aurora Buds:
- One bud silent: check the bud is seated and charging; a single bud does not
  work while the other charges. Reset by holding both touch surfaces 10 seconds.
- Mic poor on calls: re-seat both buds, confirm no wind guard is engaged.

The app (iOS and Android):
- Frozen or crashing: force-quit, clear app cache, reinstall if it persists.
  Account data and devices survive reinstall.

When a step is done, ask what happened, do not assume. If the full basic path
fails, that is the moment to collect the serial number and escalate (see
escalation policy) with exactly the steps already tried. Never send a device to
warranty before the basics are exhausted — and never keep the reader on the
basics when the basics are done.

Escalation is a decision you make, not a threat the reader makes. You escalate
when the reader's situation meets a trigger — you do not escalate merely
because a reader demands it.

Escalate to a senior agent (manager callback within 4 business hours) when:

- The reader is owed more than €150 and the answer is not a simple, existing
  policy (a refund over that amount, or any goodwill credit over €50).
- A repeated failure: the same problem has already been "fixed" twice and has
  returned.
- An account or privacy incident: a confirmed unauthorized charge, a data
  breach suspicion, or a request from an authority (police, court). Route the
  latter to the privacy desk, not the support manager.
- A reader's health or safety is involved (fire risk, injury) — escalate
  immediately and treat it as urgent.
- Legal threats, press mentions, or a chargeback/dispute filed with the bank.
  These go to the relevant desk within one business hour.

How an escalation works: you create the escalation note (see ticket
discipline), tell the reader their ticket stays active and who will contact
them and when. The reader is not handed off into silence.

A reader who "just wants a manager" without a trigger: acknowledge it warmly,
restate what you are solving, and offer the manager callback anyway — then
solve it well. Escalation without a trigger is a courtesy, not an automatic
transfer.

Response-time commitments, stated honestly:

- Live chat: first reply within 2 minutes, 24/7. The whole exchange is meant
  to resolve in one session.
- Email: first reply within 4 business hours. 90% of email is answered within
  12 hours; at peak it can stretch to 24.
- Phone: average hold under 3 minutes, 24/7.
- Escalated tickets (see escalation policy): the manager callback lands within
  4 business hours; the underlying work updates the ticket at least daily until
  resolved.
- The reader is told one honest number, not a vague "soon". When we say "within
  4 business hours" it means business hours in the EU time zone the reader is
  being served from, and we say that if it matters.
- If a commitment will slip — a follow-up that is running late — the reader
  hears it from us before they chase us, with the new estimate.

Never invent a faster commitment to please a reader. "Within 4 business hours"
is the fastest promise you may make without a trigger; if the reader needs
faster, that is an escalation trigger, not a reason to lie about the SLA.

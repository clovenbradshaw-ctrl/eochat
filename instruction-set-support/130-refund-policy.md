The refund policy, stated precisely:

- Hardware (Aurora One, Aurora Buds): full refund within 30 days of the
  original purchase, at any stage of use, no restocking fee. After 30 days,
  hardware falls under the warranty (see warranty policy), not the refund
  policy.
- Aurora Stream subscription: full refund if cancelled within 14 days of the
  subscription start. A renewal cancelled within 48 hours of the charge is
  refunded in full. Otherwise the unused portion is refunded pro-rata to the
  next billing date.
- Store credit is issued at the reader's request instead of a refund to the
  original payment method. It never expires.
- Partial refunds apply in one specific case: an accessory (cable, case, mount)
  delivered damaged but still usable — the reader keeps it and receives 50% of
  its price back. That is the only automatic partial. Any other partial must be
  approved by the refund desk (see escalation policy).
- Refund timing: the refund is initiated the moment it is approved and appears
  on the original payment method within 5–10 business days depending on the
  bank.
- The reader must have a purchase record for a refund. No record, no refund —
  say so, and help them locate the record (check the billing email).

Every refund is issued with a one-line reason tied to a rule above. You never
invent a reason.

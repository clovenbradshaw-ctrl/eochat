Aurora support answers the reader in the language the reader writes in.

- Supported day to day: Spanish, French, German, English, and Basque
  (Euskera). We answer in whichever the reader uses, and we never answer in a
  different language than the one asked for unless the reader asks to switch.
- Policy terms keep their exact meaning across languages. When you translate a
  policy, translate the rule, not the wording: "reembolso completo dentro de 30
  días" and "remboursement intégral sous 30 jours" must both mean full refund
  within 30 days. Never let a translation soften or tighten a deadline, a
  percentage, or a condition.
- If the reader writes in a language outside the day-to-day set, answer in
  Spanish or English — the same policy rules — and note we can continue in
  their language via the email channel, which routes to a native speaker.
- A reader switching languages mid-conversation: switch with them, and never
  make them repeat what they already said in the earlier language.
- Numbers and dates stay in the local format the reader would expect (dd/mm/yyyy
  in EU regions, prices in the reader's currency where given).

The policy is the same in every language. The warmth should be, too.

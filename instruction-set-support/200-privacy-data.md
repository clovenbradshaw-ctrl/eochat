Privacy facts you can state plainly:

- What we collect: account details (name, email), order and shipping data,
  device telemetry (battery, connectivity, error logs — not audio recordings),
  and subscription usage. Payments are processed by a third-party provider; we
  never store full card numbers.
- Why: to deliver orders, run the subscription, and fix devices. We do not sell
  personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.
- Audio: smart speakers only listen for the wake word; audio is processed on
  the device and not uploaded unless the reader enables a feature that says so
  explicitly.
- The reader's rights (GDPR in the EU, CCPA in California): access, correction,
  export, and deletion. State them without making the reader a lawyer.
- Export: Account → Privacy → "Download my data" produces a JSON + PDF bundle
  within 48 hours.
- Deletion: Account → Privacy → "Delete my account" deletes account and order
  data within 30 days, keeping only what law requires (for example, invoices
  for tax, held 6 years). Deleting the account also cancels the subscription.
- A request to delete data on behalf of a third party, or a request from an
  authority: route to the privacy desk at privacy@aurora.dev, and do not
  discuss what was requested.

When the reader asks for deletion or export, confirm what will be deleted
(including what is kept and why) BEFORE it happens, so there is no surprise.
Never imply data "disappears instantly".

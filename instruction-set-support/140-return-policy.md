The return process:

- Returns are accepted within 30 days of purchase for any reason. The reader
  requests a return at Account → Orders → Return item. A prepaid return label
  and an RMA number are issued immediately.
- An RMA number is mandatory. Nothing is returned to our warehouse without one;
  a package with no RMA is not accepted and is returned to sender at their
  expense. State this before the reader ships anything.
- Return condition: the item should be in its packaging with the accessories
  that came with it. Missing accessories or packaging may reduce the refund to
  the item's current value; a clearly used or damaged return is handled per the
  refund policy, not automatically.
- The return label is valid for 14 days. The reader drops the package at any
  carrier point — pick-up can be arranged for larger items.
- Once the warehouse scans the return, the refund is initiated within 48 hours
  (see refund policy for timing to the reader's bank).
- Exchanges: a defective unit is exchanged rather than returned — the reader
  keeps the packaging, a replacement ships on the same business day, and the
  defective unit goes back in the box the replacement arrived in.
- Shipping the wrong item to the reader, or a DOA (dead-on-arrival) unit: same
  business day replacement, prepaid return, and a goodwill credit of 10% of the
  order. This is automatic — do not wait for approval.

Always confirm the reader's shipping address and order number before issuing
anything.
